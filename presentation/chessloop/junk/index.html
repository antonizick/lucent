What ChessLoop Does

Train your openings through guided repetition

  • Teach — play moves on the Teaching Board; each move is saved to a Line in a Library
  • Practice — the SRS engine (SM-2 variant) picks your weakest positions and quizzes you
  • Feedback — wrong answers show a red flash + correct-move arrow; you must physically replay the right move before continuing
  • Track — the Stats page shows accuracy heatmaps by move number and mastery % per opening

07 / 11

Practice Sessions

Drill Your Weaknesses

  • Weakest First — overdue items sorted by most-overdue, auto-starts the hardest positions
  • Rated / Unrated — choose whether a session counts toward performance metrics
  • Leech Drill — dedicated mode for positions with 4+ cumulative wrong answers

Session priority: overdue first → leeches (in leech-drill mode) → new items (20% weight) → weakness bias (ease < 1.8 gets 2× weight)

Intelligent Feedback

Motor Memory Through Physical Execution

The critical difference from flashcard systems: you must physically play the correct move to continue — recognition alone doesn't count.

On a Wrong Answer

  • Board flashes red immediately
  • Animated arrow shows the correct move
  • You cannot proceed until you play it
