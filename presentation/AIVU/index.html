Introducing AIVU

What It Is

AIVU is your personal AI-powered Virtual University that works with you, not just for you. It combines:

  • Collaborative curriculum and lesson plan design
  • An always-available 1:1 AI Professor — no judgment, infinite patience
  • A powerful adaptive spaced-repetition testing engine
  • A personalized progress dashboard
  • Full support for your own documents (RAG) and custom content

Key differentiator: not just another chatbot — a complete, structured learning ecosystem you control.

How AIVU Works

  • Edit curriculum or request new focus areas, anytime
